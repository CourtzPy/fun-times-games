Install

Three
steps.

  1. Download the file

    Save the world or the addon. You need Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.100 or newer.

  2. Open it in Minecraft

    On a phone or tablet, choose Open in Minecraft. On a computer, double-click the file. A world is added to your worlds. An addon is added to your packs.

  3. Play

    One Block starts you on a grass block over the void. For Most Tools Z, turn on both Most Tools Z packs in the world settings, then take the gear from the Equipment tab.
